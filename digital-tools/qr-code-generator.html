    Free Custom QR Code Generator

    Create a QR code people can scan with confidence

    The builder stays simple at the top. This guide below explains the details when you need them.

    What you can create

    • Website and landing-page QR codes
    • WhatsApp chats with an optional message
    • Wi-Fi access without typing the password
    • Email, phone, SMS, contact and location QR codes

    How to get a reliable result

    1. Choose the correct QR type.
    2. Enter complete and accurate details.
    3. Keep strong contrast between the QR code and background.
    4. Add a small, clear logo only when needed.
    5. Test the QR code with a phone before sharing or printing.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this QR Code Generator free?

    Yes. It is free to use, requires no account, and generates the QR code in your browser.

    Can I create a QR code with a logo?

    Yes. Open the Logo step, upload an image, select square, rounded or circular, and adjust the logo size.

    Can I add text above and below the QR code?

    Yes. Open Poster Text to use editable smart suggestions, switch either line on or off, and optionally show athanasinspires.com at the bottom.

    Should I download the poster or QR code only?

    Choose the A4 PDF poster for professional printing, poster PNG for digital sharing, or QR Only for a clean PNG without text and the poster frame.

    Why might a QR code fail to scan?

    Low colour contrast, incomplete details, an oversized logo, a damaged image, or printing it too small can make scanning difficult. Use the Test QR button before sharing.
