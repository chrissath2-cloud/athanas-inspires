A strong foundation

What you'll learn in Session 1

From opening the program to running your first presentation, this lesson covers the complete beginner journey.

01Understand PowerPoint

Know the difference between the program, the presentation, and slides.

02Know your workspace

Explore tabs, the Ribbon, the Slides Pane, placeholders, and layouts.

03Create your first slides

Follow the three-slide “About Me” demonstration step by step.

04Manage slides

Duplicate, move, and delete slides with confidence.

05Run the presentation

See your work from the audience's perspective.

06Save and reopen

Save your project, find the file again, and continue later.
