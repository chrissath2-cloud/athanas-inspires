    Simple Workflow

    From Information to a Finished QR Design in Four Steps

    The generator is built for beginners, but it still provides the controls needed for professional school, church, event, business, and community designs.

    1

    Choose the purpose

    Select a website, WhatsApp, Wi-Fi, contact, payment, social-media, or another QR type.

    2

    Enter and verify

    Add the exact information and carefully confirm phone numbers, links, locations, and payment details.

    3

    Design safely

    Apply a premium theme, colours, logo, title, message, and layout while watching the readiness score.

    4

    Test and export

    Scan the code, then download PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF, or a print-ready layout.

    ⚠
    Important: QR codes can open links or display information, but Athanas Inspires does not verify destinations entered by users. Test every QR code and confirm sensitive details before printing, sharing, or paying.
